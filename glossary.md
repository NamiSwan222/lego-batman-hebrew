Instructions use the masculine imperative (לחץ, גש, החזק), as most Hebrew game translations do.

## What stays as it is (on purpose)

- **Button tags** `[SPECIAL]`, `[ACTION]`, `[[cross]]`: the game draws them as button icons.
- **Key names** ("Enter", "Esc", "Space"): shown as printed on the keyboard.
- **People's names in the credits** (name/credit/thanks lines), and company names: Traveller's
  Tales, TT Games Publishing, Warner Bros. Interactive Entertainment, DC Comics. Job titles and
  headings are translated.
- **Licence notices** in the credits (Bink copyright, the Ogg Vorbis BSD licence). The licence
  requires its text to be reproduced as written.
- **Brand marks**: "LEGO®", "TT", "FSAA" (inside a Hebrew label), "Wii", "Windows".

## Writing rules

- Write normal (logical) Hebrew. The build reverses it for the game.
- No niqqud and no Hebrew punctuation (geresh/gershayim/maqaf): the game font has only the
  27 letters. Use `'` for geresh (ג'וקר), `''` for gershayim (מפכ''ל) and `-` for maqaf.
- Never use a double quote `"`. Inside a line, quotes are `''`.
- `\n` forces a line break. Long lines are wrapped automatically (about 40 characters).
